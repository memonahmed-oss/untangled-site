Untangled Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 15, 2026

Untangled ("the app", "we") is an iPhone app that helps you keep track of tasks, homework, events, and your school day. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It is operated by Ahmed Memon ("we"). Contact: untangledsupportteam@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your sign-in details and the things you put in the app.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • There are no analytics or advertising tools in the app.
  • You can delete your account and all of your data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhat it isWhy
Account informationWhen you sign in with Google: your email address, the name you choose to use in the app, and a unique account ID. We do not receive your Google password.To create your account, sign you in, and greet you by name.
Your contentEverything you enter: tasks, homework, tests, events, reminders, birthdays, titles, notes, subjects, places, dates and times, repeat rules, completion status, and planned work sessions.To run the app and keep your items in sync between your devices.
Your settingsYour School schedule (classes and times), sleep schedule, reminder and planning preferences, and similar choices.To plan your day and keep your settings when you change phones.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, health data, financial information, or advertising identifier.

2. Information stored only on your phone

Some data never leaves your device: notification scheduling, the "last time" durations the app remembers for your work sessions, one-time tips you have seen, and a local copy of your items used by the app and its widgets and Lock Screen / Dynamic Island Live Activity (shared with the app through an on-device App Group container). Notifications are scheduled locally on your phone; we do not send push notifications from a server.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide and improve the app's features: signing you in, storing and syncing your items and settings, scheduling reminders on your device, planning your day, and showing weather. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your information. We use the following service providers, who process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — our backend. It hosts sign-in and the database that stores your account, items, and settings. Your data is protected so each account can only read and write its own rows.
  • Google — used only for "Sign in with Google". Google tells us your email address and name. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — the weather service. If weather is shown, the app sends it the latitude and longitude of your location (or the city you picked) and receives a forecast. It does not receive your name, email, or any of your items.
  • Apple — the app uses Apple's system services, including place lookup (to turn a city name into coordinates and coordinates into a city name), notifications, and iCloud/device backups, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the service.

5. Tracking

Untangled does not track you. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tools that follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and content for as long as your account exists. When you delete an item it is marked deleted so your other devices can learn of the deletion; it is removed for good when you delete your account. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your account and all of your items from our servers.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and your synced data from our servers and erases the app's data on that phone.
  • Sign out: Settings → Sign Out. Your data stays in your account for the next time you sign in.
  • Location: you can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Untangled, or choose a city manually in the app.
  • Notifications: manage them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Untangled.
  • Access or correction: your items and settings are visible and editable inside the app. For anything else, or if you cannot use the in-app deletion, email us at untangledsupportteam@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or to object to processing. Contact us to use them. Where GDPR applies, our legal basis is performing the service you asked for (account and sync) and your consent (location).

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in is stored in the iOS Keychain. Our database allows each account to access only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Untangled is designed for students and may be used by teenagers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. [CONFIRM: your intended minimum age. Sign-in with Google already requires users to meet Google's age requirements.]

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States or other countries. By using the app you understand your information may be transferred to and stored in countries other than your own. [CONFIRM: your Supabase project's region.]

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact
